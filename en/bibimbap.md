## 👨‍🍳 How to Cook
1. Prepare various vegetables (carrots, spinach, mushrooms).
2. Stir-fry vegetables separately with a pinch of salt.
3. Place rice in a bowl and top with the prepared vegetables and Gochujang.
4. Add a fried egg on top and a drizzle of sesame oil.
